## 📦 Installation (3 Simple Steps)

### Step 1: Open Chrome Extensions

1. Open Google Chrome
2. Navigate to: `chrome://extensions/`
3. Enable **"Developer mode"** (toggle in top-right corner)

### Step 2: Load Extension

1. Click **"Load unpacked"** button
2. Navigate to this directory: `Lead-Generation-`
3. Click **"Select Folder"**

### Step 3: Pin to Toolbar (Optional but Recommended)

1. Click the **puzzle icon** (🧩) in Chrome toolbar
2. Find **"Lead Generator Pro"**
3. Click the **pin icon** (📌)

## 🐛 Troubleshooting

### Extension Not Appearing?
- Make sure you enabled "Developer mode"
- Refresh the extensions page
- Check for errors in red text

### No Data Extracted?
- Refresh the webpage
- Click extension icon → "Extract Leads"
- Some pages may not have detectable contact info

### Icons Not Showing?
- This should be fixed now!
- If needed, run: `python3 simple-icon-generator.py`
- Reload extension in chrome://extensions/

### Sidebar Not Opening?
- Look for floating "🎯 LEADS" button on right side
- Or click "Open Sidebar" in popup
- Try refreshing the page
